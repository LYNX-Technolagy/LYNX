Privacy Policy

LYNX Technology respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to our customers. Here's what we collect:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, and company name when you contact us.
  • Usage Data: How you interact with our website, including pages visited and time spent.
  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, and device type for analytics.
  • Cookies: We use cookies to improve your experience and analyze site traffic.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information helps us provide better service. We use it to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide support
  • Process service requests and transactions
  • Send updates about our services (only with your consent)
  • Improve our website and services through analytics
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Data Sharing

We don't sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Service Providers: Trusted third parties who help us operate our business (e.g., hosting, email delivery).
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights.
  • Business Transfers: If LYNX Technology is acquired or merges with another company.

🔒 Your Data Is Safe

We never sell your personal information to third parties. Your trust is important to us.

4. Data Security

We take data security seriously. We implement industry-standard measures to protect your information:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/SSL)
  • Secure storage with restricted access
  • Regular security updates and monitoring
  • Employee training on data protection

5. Your Rights

You have control over your personal information. You can:

  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of marketing communications

To exercise these rights, contact us and we'll respond within 30 days.

6. Cookies

We use cookies to enhance your experience. Cookies help us:

  • Remember your preferences
  • Analyze site traffic and usage
  • Improve our services based on user behavior

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. However, this may affect some website features.

7. Children's Privacy

LYNX Technology does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us and we'll delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll post changes on this page and update the "Last Updated" date at the top.
