Huddle

Finding a pickup game shouldn't be harder than playing in one.

If you've ever tried to organise a casual game of basketball or football, you know the pain. Someone creates a WhatsApp group. Someone else suggests a time. People drop out. The location changes. Half the group doesn't see the message. By the time you actually play, you're exhausted from the admin, not the sport.

Huddle is the solution to that chaos. It's a pickup sports discovery app that connects players with local games happening right now, taking the admin out of organising and putting the focus back on playing.

How it works

Huddle is built around three core pillars: discover, join, and play. The app uses localised discovery to show you games happening near you, complete with skill levels, player counts, and real-time confirmation.

Local Discovery

Find pickup games in your area, filtered by sport and skill level.

Real-time Rosters

See exactly who's playing and how many spots are left.

Native Performance

Built entirely in React Native for iOS and Android.

Zero Admin

Automated reminders and updates. No more messy group chats.

The Tech & Design

Huddle was built end-to-end by LYNX. It features an anime/manga-inspired visual identity that breaks away from the sterile, corporate look of most fitness apps. The current Beta 1 release is live and usable today — not a mockup.

Brief

The fastest way to get a project brief from a client, without the email chains.

Freelancers and agencies waste hours going back and forth with clients just to get a single paragraph of project requirements. You ask for a brief, they send a WhatsApp voice note, you have to transcribe it, they forgot the budget, you have to ask again.

Brief fixes this. It's a simple tool that gives your client a structured form to fill out. They answer the right questions once, you get a clean PDF or email. No more chasing.

Why we built it

We were building websites for clients and constantly running into the same problem. Clients would say "I want a website," but wouldn't know what pages, what content, or what the goal was. We built Brief as an internal tool, realised other agencies were struggling with the same thing, and decided to turn it into a product.

Structured Intake

Clients answer the exact questions you need answered, every time.

Clean Output

Generate a professional-looking PDF brief from their answers.

Save Hours

Stop the email ping-pong. Get what you need on the first try.

Zero Setup

No signup needed for the client. They just fill the form and submit.
