Our Story

LYNX Technology is a newly founded startup built by a core team of experienced senior engineers. Each of us has spent years solving hard problems at scale for Fortune 500 companies and leading startups, and we decided to start fresh with a single mission.

We're not here to follow industry patterns or deliver "good enough" solutions. We're building for teams that refuse to compromise, who want engineering excellence as their competitive advantage.

Remote-first and lean, we're focused on partnering with a select few projects where we can make an outsized impact. Every line of code, every architectural decision, every deployment reflects our commitment to precision and excellence.
