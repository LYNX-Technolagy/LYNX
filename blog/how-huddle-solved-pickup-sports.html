Case Study: How Huddle solved the pickup sports admin nightmare

Published 25 October 2026

If you've ever tried to organise a casual game of basketball or football, you know the pain. Someone creates a WhatsApp group. Someone else suggests a time. People drop out. The location changes. Half the group doesn't see the message. By the time you actually play, you're exhausted from the admin, not the sport.

We built Huddle to fix this. It's a pickup sports discovery app that connects players with local games happening right now, taking the admin out of organising and putting the focus back on playing. You can try the Beta here.

The Problem

Before writing a single line of code, we interviewed local players in East London. The feedback was consistent: the admin was killing the fun. People wanted to play, but they didn't want to be the 'organiser' who had to chase 15 people for confirmation.

The Solution

We built Huddle using React Native for a single codebase that runs on both iOS and Android. The app uses localised discovery to show you games happening near you, complete with skill levels, player counts, and real-time confirmation. We added an anime/manga-inspired visual identity to break away from the sterile, corporate look of most fitness apps.

The Tech Stack

  • Frontend: React Native (iOS & Android)
  • Backend: Supabase for real-time database and auth
  • Design: Custom manga-inspired UI/UX

The Result

Huddle is currently live in Beta 1. The feedback has been incredible — players are finding games they never knew existed, and organisers are spending less time chasing confirmations and more time playing. This is the level of quality we bring to every client project at LYNX Full-Stack.
