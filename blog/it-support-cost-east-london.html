What IT support actually costs in East London

Published 2 November 2026

If you call an IT support company in East London, the first question they usually ask is, 'What's your budget?' That's a red flag. You shouldn't have to bid for a service like a broken printer or a slow server. You need to know the price upfront.

At LYNX, we publish our pricing. Here is a breakdown of our IT support costs, and what you actually get for the money.

Pay As You Go: R550 per visit

This is for a one-off support session (60–90 minutes). It includes diagnosis and the fix, whether remote or on-site in East London. It's perfect if you just need a printer set up or a virus removed once.

Starter Retainer: R1,350 per month

If your business relies on computers, you need priority. The Starter Retainer gives you up to 3 support sessions per month, priority response (within 2 hours), and a free monthly system check. It's cheaper than paying for 3 visits individually.

Business Retainer: R2,200 per month

This is for businesses with multiple machines and a higher risk of downtime. You get up to 6 support sessions, priority response within 1 hour, and a dedicated support contact. If you have 5+ employees, this is almost always the right plan.

The hidden costs to watch out for

When comparing IT companies in East London, watch out for 'call-out fees.' Some companies charge R500 just to arrive, before they even diagnose the issue. Our prices are fixed. You pay R550 for the session, whether it takes 20 minutes or 90.
