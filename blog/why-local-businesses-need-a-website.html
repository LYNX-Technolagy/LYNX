Why your East London business needs a website (not just Facebook)

Published 8 November 2026

A lot of local businesses in East London run entirely off Facebook and WhatsApp. And it makes sense — it's free, it's easy, and everyone has an account. But relying solely on social media is like building your business on rented land. You don't own it.

At LYNX Full-Stack, we build websites for local businesses. Here is why a Facebook page alone isn't enough.

1. Social media is rented land

If Facebook changes its algorithm tomorrow, your reach could drop to zero. If your account gets hacked or suspended, you lose contact with every customer. A website is yours. You control it.

2. Google doesn't rank Facebook pages

When someone in East London searches for 'plumber near me' or 'web designer East London', Google prioritises websites. Facebook pages are often buried. If you don't have a website, you're invisible to the most important search engine in the world.

3. Professionalism and trust

A clean, well-designed website tells a customer that you are established and professional. It gives you a place to showcase your work, your team, and your story without being squeezed into a feed. Learn more about why our clients choose us.

4. You own the customer relationship

A website lets you capture email addresses, run contact forms, and own the data. Social media platforms keep that data. When you own the channel, you build a long-term asset.
