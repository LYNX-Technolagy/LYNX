Practical AI for local businesses: What actually works in 2026

Published 12 November 2026

If you read the news, you'd think AI is either going to solve all our problems or end civilization. For a local business owner in East London, neither of those things is particularly useful. What's useful is knowing how to use AI to save an hour a day on admin.

Here is what's actually working for our clients right now.

Ready for a tech upgrade?

We help local businesses figure out the right tools. Sometimes it's a simple automation, sometimes it's a custom web app.

Talk to us

1. Drafting emails and proposals

Tools like ChatGPT or Claude are surprisingly good at drafting the first version of a quote, a proposal, or a difficult email. It's not about letting the AI write it — it's about giving you a starting point to edit, which cuts writing time in half.

2. Customer service bots (that don't suck)

If your business gets the same 10 questions every day, a simple AI chatbot on your website can answer them. We build these using tools like Voiceflow or simple API integrations. It's not about replacing a human — it's about freeing up your receptionist to handle the complex questions.

3. Image editing

If you sell products online, AI photo editing tools (like removing backgrounds or enhancing lighting) can save hours on product photography. It's no longer necessary to hire a photographer for every single item.

4. Automating admin

Tools like Zapier or Make can connect your inbox to your CRM or spreadsheet. When a quote is accepted, it automatically creates an invoice. This is AI in the background, saving you time without you having to think about it.

What AI can't do

AI cannot replace a local relationship. It cannot shake a client's hand or fix a laptop. But it can give you back the time to do those things. That's the real value for a small business.
